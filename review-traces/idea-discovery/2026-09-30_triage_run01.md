## 3. TOP PICKS

**TOP PICKS: diffuser-class-statistical-distance-law, gt-bandwidth-mismatch-hallucination-ablation, memory-effect-knee-vs-training-diversity**

**diffuser-class-statistical-distance-law**  
This is the foundational one. The field says “unseen diffuser generalization” without an operational definition of “same class.” A compact law — even a negative one — would force precision. Convincing result: one or two statistical distances (correlation length, height distribution, spectral shape) predict validation transfer loss with high accuracy and a changepoint, or a clean failure showing no low-dimensional law. Either way, the paper becomes the cite-for-definitions reference.

**gt-bandwidth-mismatch-hallucination-ablation**  
It attacks the word “hallucination” causally. The pilot’s 3.1× out-of-band suppression is striking, and the PSNR-reversal under reference bandwidth is a protocol result in itself. Convincing result: a fine-tune-length sweep reproduces the out-of-band effect with bootstrap-stable CIs, and the in-band PSNR drop is shown to be an information-band tradeoff, not under-training. That turns a MIXED pilot into a reference protocol for measuring hallucination.

**memory-effect-knee-vs-training-diversity**  
This is the high-upside pick. If the object-extent generalization curve kinks at the memory-effect angle after statistically matching training diversity, physics — not dataset statistics — is the dominant prior. If the curve decays smoothly, much “memory-effect” language in DL papers is decorative. Convincing result: a changepoint at ME width with posterior probability >0.95 in a variance decomposition; or a clean null monotone result. Both are papers that change how results are interpreted.

## 5. PILOT DESIGN CAVEATS

- **CPU simulation scale**: 6 px speckle grain and 12 px MTF support are narrow; real speckle statistics and CNN hallucinations may differ at larger grain/receiver sizes. Repeat top candidates at 2–3 px and 20+ px grains.
- **400 test images** are adequate for mean PSNR but not for tail hallucination events or rare failure modes. Report bootstrap CIs and, for dichotomy results (changepoints, crossovers), use posterior distributions, not point estimates.
- **Fine-tune length** is a suspected moderator for Idea 2. The 3.1× effect may shrink or vanish with longer training. Force a sweep or pre-register early stopping before interpreting the MIXED verdict.
- **Reference-dependence sub-finding**: PSNR ranks flipping with reference bandwidth is a property of the metric, not the method. Pre-register primary endpoints — band-limited PSNR, out-of-band energy ratio, in-band PSNR — to avoid moving goalposts.
- **Hallucination-energy ratio** must be validated on synthetic known hallucinations; otherwise it may conflate blur, edge sharpening, and true out-of-band fabrication.
- **Synthetic diffuser-only simulation** is too kind; at least one volumetric or experimentally measured speckle check should be included before submission for any top-ranking law or crossover.
- **Statistical-distance laws** fitted to one simulator family may overfit. Use held-out diffuser families and different phase-screen generators.
